## 🌟 Overview

**DiabPredict** is an AI-powered diabetes risk prediction system designed to help users understand their potential diabetes risk through an interactive mobile application.

The system combines **Machine Learning**, **Explainable AI (XAI)**, **OCR**, and a **local Arabic rule-based health assistant** into one privacy-oriented platform.

Instead of providing only a prediction such as *"Diabetic"* or *"Non-Diabetic"*, DiabPredict provides:

- 🎯 Diabetes risk probability
- 📊 Risk category
- 🔍 Explainable prediction factors
- 🧪 Automatic extraction of HbA1c and Blood Glucose
- 💬 Arabic health guidance
- 📈 Prediction history
- 👨‍⚕️ Doctor-oriented patient management
- 🔒 Local and privacy-oriented processing

> ⚠️ **Medical Disclaimer:** DiabPredict is an educational and risk-awareness tool. It is not a certified medical diagnostic system and should not replace professional medical advice, laboratory testing, or consultation with a qualified healthcare professional.
